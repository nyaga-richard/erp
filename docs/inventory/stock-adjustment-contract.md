Status: VERIFIED for reviewed single-product gains/losses/full reversals only. Inventory overall remains IN_PROGRESS. Initial workflow: a single STOCK product/base-unit line in one active scoped warehouse. This is a reviewed stock adjustment, not a purchase/receipt, sale, tax-calculation workflow, transfer or opening-balance import. STOCK_GAIN records proposed positive quantity and a documented positive total functional-currency value; that value is a proposal requiring review, not posting authority. STOCK_LOSS accepts quantity only and derives its cent value from locked stock. Neither accepts a balance, unit cost, account, actor or tax amount. Zero-cent adjustments are refused until zero-financial-effect source semantics are implemented; no fake zero journal.

DRAFT → PENDING_APPROVAL → APPROVED → POSTED, or rejection/cancellation. Submission locks the pool and freezes exact before/after valuation, current pool version, rule selection, materialized lines and policy snapshot. Creator may refresh an unposted valuation by incrementing the source revision, discarding approval authority (not history). Changed quantity/value requires cancellation and a new attributed draft in this first immutable-line workflow. Concurrent stock changes make submission stale; posting fails closed and requires a fresh valuation and approval.

Reuse the central workflow with configurable published STOCK_GAIN/STOCK_LOSS threshold policies; absent policy uses company segregation flags and inventory:approve, not journals:approve. Require explicit inventory:view/create/submit/approve/post/cancel and inventory:cost:view; all valued workflow reads/writes require cost visibility. No role-name authorization. Initial demo grants are explicit; no operational balances/rules are seeded.

DB contracts: normalized immutable adjustment line; immutable versioned submitted valuation; existing source/approval/journal/movement/projection tables reused. Single-pool advisory locks serialize absent and existing balances. Movement insert must match the approved current valuation, same actor/source/revision/date/warehouse/product, exact projection version and original approval. Projection writes are trigger-derived, never granted as editable balances. Reject source dates earlier than the pool's latest valued date. SQL recomputes value/quantity deltas independently and binds posted stock-control lines to reviewed symbolic mappings. Complete source, rule application, movement, projection, journal, event/audit and idempotency result commit together. Deliberate later failure rolls all back.

Reuse the existing journal writer through an internal prepared-line method; the manual HTTP route remains non-control-only. Posting uses the reviewed submitted mapping, rechecks current applicability/account eligibility and freezes source/rule/valuation evidence. Retirement cannot invalidate an applied rule because journal_posting_rule_applications is populated. Gain: Dr inventory / Cr reviewed gain account; Loss: Dr reviewed loss account / Cr inventory. No tax or AR/AP effects.

Read endpoints are branch-scoped, bounded search/pagination and movement drilldown. Legacy manual-journal document routes must not expose inventory source payloads to unrelated readers. Product purchase reference price is not used. Audit costs require inventory cost visibility; general financial-ledger access still permits authorized GL amounts, not arbitrary product/stock payloads.

Full reversal implemented: a separately numbered STOCK_REVERSAL draft links the original posted gain/loss. Submission freezes the exact negation of original quantity/value and GL mapping, not current average. Original rule may be retired; current accounts must remain eligible. Original-source lock and unique active reversal prevent duplicates; cancelled requests may be replaced. No reversal chains. Native checks bind original movement/journal/rule identity and journal.reversal_of. Nonnegative resulting quantity/value and zero-quantity/zero-value consistency remain mandatory. Partial returns, transfers, zero-value stock, serial/batch/expiry, original-cost supplier/customer returns and multi-line adjustments remain later contracts. No posted rows may be edited/deleted meanwhile.
