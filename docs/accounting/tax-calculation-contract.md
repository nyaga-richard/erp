# Tax calculation kernel — dependency work, not a released tax module

The existing journal/approval engine remains the posting authority. This work adds a pure exact-decimal calculation component while configuration/numbering and operational services are still being completed. It does not expose an endpoint accepting client-selected tax rates, seed legal rates, create tax ledger rows or claim eTIMS readiness.

Inputs: strict decimal strings for quantity/unit price/discount; business date; company currency; explicit category classification; authoritative effective-dated rate snapshots now supplied by the permission-checked informational configuration preview; operational resolution remains unfinished. Rates are fractional factors (e.g. 0.16 in tests), not hard-coded jurisdiction constants. Current ledger scale is two decimal places, matching numeric(20,2); unsupported scales fail rather than silently truncating. Products/quantities/unit prices may have six decimal places, rates/recoverability ten. No JavaScript floating-point money arithmetic.

For TAXABLE, require at least one positive configured rate. ZERO_RATED requires explicit zero-rate snapshots; EXEMPT and NON_TAXABLE require no applied rates and preserve their distinct classification. Duplicate rate/tax identifiers and out-of-date snapshots fail. All components use the same net base; compound taxes are explicitly unsupported until a reviewed rule model is implemented. All applied snapshots must agree on inclusive/exclusive mode. Discount is a currency amount applied to the rounded line extension, before tax, and cannot exceed that extension.

Exclusive: round line extension, deduct discount, compute each component from net and round half-up to cents; gross = net + component taxes. Inclusive: rounded discounted extension is gross; compute unrounded net by dividing by one plus the sum of rates, then round net half-up. Tax = gross − net. Allocate that exact tax total using largest remainders over each unrounded component, with stable tax-ID/rate-ID tie-breaking; never leave a hidden discrepancy. Recoverable tax is rounded from component tax × configured recoverable fraction; nonrecoverable tax is the explicit remainder.

Output includes engine version, rounding/allocation policy, original normalized quantity/unit price/discount, full effective rate IDs/dates/factors/recoverability, classification, currency, price mode, net/tax/gross, component breakdown and recoverable/nonrecoverable amounts. Returned snapshot and nested structures are frozen. Serializing this snapshot must retain enough information to reproduce the calculation; later rate changes cannot alter it. Reject final values outside the current numeric(20,2) range.

Tests cover inclusive/exclusive, zero/exempt/non-taxable distinction, partial recoverability, effective boundaries, invalid dates/rates/float input/discount/duplicate IDs, rounding residual distribution, order-independent calculations, snapshot replay, and many small-value/rate combinations proving net + tax = gross and recoverable + nonrecoverable = tax. Finite-schedule database configuration, independent approval and ID-based preview now also have database/HTTP/browser evidence; see tax-configuration-next.md. Tax registers, retirement/amendment, return allocation and operational accounting integration remain unfinished, not verified on the strength of calculator tests.
