## Initial bounded workflow

An authenticated customer proposer submits an immutable initial registration request. PENDING → APPLIED or REJECTED by a different authenticated customer approver, regardless of role name. Current membership, permission and company eligibility are checked again inside the serialized command. A proposal/rejection has no customer publication, ledger, journal, stock or tax effect. Approval atomically publishes the exact requested customer, its reviewed AR-control binding where applicable, the decision and complete native audit. There are no editable balances or opening-balance fields.

Company-scoped unique uppercase customer number is a business-master identifier, not a financial document number. Name, optional phone/email/address/KRA PIN reference, REGISTERED/WALK_IN type, base-currency credit limit and payment terms are proposed data. Currency is resolved by the server from company configuration. PIN format validation does not verify registration with KRA. Optional governed tax category is a classification reference only; it never overrides invoice tax eligibility, rates, recoverability or statutory documents.

REGISTERED customers require an explicitly selected active postable leaf ASSET/AR control account in the same company. No seeded account code is selected implicitly. Zero credit limit means no authorized credit exposure, not unlimited credit. The master does not itself authorize a credit sale or implement exposure checks. WALK_IN is company-unique, has zero terms and zero limit, no AR-control binding, and no phone/email/address/PIN personal identity. It is a cash-sale classification, not an invented legal person. No financial effects occur during registration.

## Data integrity and attribution

Normalized `customer_change_requests` hold proposed fields, creator/time, reason, pending/terminal state, decision author/time/reason and the generated publication identity. Existing customers gain publication link, approver/time and version. customer_accounts is used for the explicit AR binding; it contains no balance. Same-company foreign keys constrain links. Published registration and terminal decisions are immutable, including to migration-owner ordinary UPDATE/DELETE; genuine later amendments require a separate reviewed lifecycle contract and migration.

Native triggers independently enforce current proposer/reviewer authority, independent maker/reviewer, strict initial state, immutable proposal fields, governed classification/current account eligibility, exact publication identity/fields, binding and complete audit in one transaction. The company configuration advisory lock serializes publication against account/classification changes. Duplicate numbers and WALK_IN publication races fail without duplicate masters. Idempotency retry returns the original result; changed payload conflicts. Audit corruption or any late error rolls back request/publication/binding/idempotency result together.

All financial numeric values remain canonical decimal strings in public projections and SQL-assembled snapshots. No JavaScript Number conversion. Authenticated actor is resolved by the server; no request actor, approvedBy, balance, currency, version or arbitrary metadata fields are accepted. Original proposer and independent publisher remain distinct.

## Permissions, API, UI

Implemented initial permissions: customers:view (authorized customer profile/credit-policy metadata), customers:propose, customers:approve. No implicit accounting, posting, payment or AR reporting permission. Generic audit readers without customers:view receive redacted customer snapshots to avoid exposing contact and policy data through a side route.

Implemented GET /customers, /customers/:id, /customers/changes, /customers/accounts, /customers/tax-categories: bounded server search/pagination, company scope and governed picklists. POST /customers/changes and /customers/changes/:id/decide: strict bodies, CSRF, retry-stable idempotency and independent publication. No update/delete routes in this first contract. UI follows backend/native green: catalog, proposal and independent review, attribution, explicit zero-credit meaning, loading/error/empty/success/confirmation, keyboard/mobile/dark, permission-only navigation and no financial dashboard grant.
